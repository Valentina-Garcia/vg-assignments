This project is for the Berkeley  Professional Certificate in Machine 
Learning and Artificial Intelligence Course Module 5 assignment 1. 

In it I analyze provided coupon data based on a number of factors. 
We first deep dive into coupons that were provided to go to bars. Here 
there seems to be a higher acceptance rate by people who go to a bar more 
then once a month.

I next deep dove into coupons that were proided for carry away/take out 
food. Here there is a generally high acceptance rate with little 
statistical difference between different groups. The only group that was 
statistically slightly more likely to accept was people who have take out/ 
carry away at least once a month.
